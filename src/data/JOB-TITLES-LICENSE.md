# Job titles

`job-titles.json` is built by `scripts/build-job-titles.mjs` from the O*NET 29.3 Database by the
U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA), used under the
CC BY 4.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: the occupation titles,
reported titles and alternate titles were merged, de-duplicated, trimmed of "All Other" and
"Except ..." suffixes, and a curated list of common technology and startup titles was added.
